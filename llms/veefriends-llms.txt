# VeeFriends

> VeeFriends is a character-driven collectibles and entertainment company founded by Gary Vaynerchuk (Vee Friends, LLC, New York), built around 250+ original characters that embody values like empathy, patience, and kindness. The brand spans NFT collections (Series 1, Series 2, Iconics, Book Games), Topps Chrome trading cards and a trading card game, comic books and cartoons, collectible stickers and pins, merchandise, and the Burn Island NFT-burning platform. VeeFriends publishes no public developer API surface today.
